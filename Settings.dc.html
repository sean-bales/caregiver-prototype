Settings and Texts
The Settings tab. Overview: who is signed in and for whom, rows to Device and Texts with a one-line summary each, an installation card only when the app is not installed, Terms and Privacy, Sign out with no confirmation. Texts is a pushed page: one number per login, verify by 6-digit code, consent recorded once, one switch. Verified and pending numbers are two different things. Error strings and consent copy are verbatim from the constraints doc. Header pass: case B of the Today header on every frame, sticky over the scrolling page, with the back control as the first row beneath it on Texts. For ruling: the Daniel pill (the Device entry from Today's header) is drawn on the right of the overview screens and hidden on the pushed Texts page. The consent sentence still says Legavo because it is verbatim from the constraints doc. Times are Daniel’s (Central), Sun, Sep 6; the unreported-timezone variant is drawn once on Today state 13.
