Device
Daniel’s Companion, reached from Settings and from the device controls on Today. One scroll: status and modes in the teal block, then levels, sleep schedule and live conversation. Modes use Today’s model: current state, one reverse action, and Off while the screen is normal; one pending request per switch, voice or screen; nothing else waits while it is pending; up to 50 seconds to confirm. Levels and schedule save as they change and show what was requested, never what the device applied, because nothing confirms them. Live conversation is Daniel’s setting; the copy says who can read the lines. Times are Daniel’s (Central), Sun, Sep 6; D12 carries the never-reported case.
Header pass: case A, transparent over the teal block, as on Today. Device is a pushed page, so the back row sits beneath the header and there is no Daniel pill (it would open the page you are on). Spec on Today F1.
